### 1.2 The take (backend)

1. `POST /api/order/?order_id=N` with `action=take`, plus `amount`, always (the reference sends `Number(takeAmount)`, which is 0 for a fixed order, whose field stays empty; for a range order a number in the order's currency; for an order in BTC, currency 1000, the swap case, sats divided by 10⁸, `frontend/src/models/Order.model.ts:232-243`; the serializer reads a decimal, `api/serializers.py:684`, and the view a float, `api/views.py:567`, only for a range order) and `password`, the SHA-256 hex of the password, for a private order (`OrderDetails/TakeButton.tsx:333`; `api/views.py:531-596`). Only on status 1 (`:558`); otherwise the view builds error 1046 but **does not return it** (`:595-596`, missing `return`) and falls through to `self.get` at the end (`:695`), which gives the order as it is only to a participant: anyone else gets 1044 (HTTP 403, `:269-275`), and a cancelled order 1043 (`:258-260`); 1046 is never sent. The client must read the answer to know the take did not happen.
2. Range: `min_amount ≤ amount ≤ max_amount` or error 1011 (`logics.py:190-194`, `views.py:566-570`).
3. Under a row lock on the robot (`views.py:572-584`): `validate_already_maker_or_taker` (`logics.py:75-135`) refuses (HTTP 409) a robot that makes or takes an order in statuses 0, 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 16 (errors 1000 maker / 1001 taker), that holds a live pre-take on any order (1002), or that is the **buyer** of an order in 13 or 15 (1003). **This is RoboSats' « one robot, one active order » rule.** A robot that finished (14, 5, 4, 12, 17, 18) can take again; RoboSats still advises a fresh robot per trade.
4. `Logics.take` (`logics.py:204-234`): penalty in force → error 1012 with `time_out` seconds (HTTP 403); an existing live pre-take of this robot on this order → nothing new (`:206-214`), a branch the view never reaches: `validate_already_maker_or_taker` (step 3) has already answered 1002 (HTTP 409) to a robot with a live pre-take on any order, this one included, so a second take while the first pre-take lives fails with 1002; else a `TakeOrder` row (`api/models/take_order.py`) with `expires_at = now + t_to_expire(TAK)` and the amount. **Several robots may pre-take the same order at once**: the first whose bond locks wins (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`: `finalize_contract` if still public, else the late bond is cancelled, i.e. returned).
5. The view answers with `self.get` (`views.py:593`): the GET of a pre-taker on a public order returns `status: 3`, `total_secs_exp`, the fiat `amount`, and the bond: `bond_invoice`, `bond_satoshis`, `expires_at` (`views.py:381-396`, `logics.py:1383-1467`). The bond is generated on the first such GET and returned again on the next ones (`:1393-1399`). **Bond amount** = `int(satoshis_now(order, take amount) × bond_size / 100)` (`:1402-1404`), `bond_size` 3 % by default, 2-15 % (`settings.py:303-305`). For a participant, the pre-taker included, the answer's `satoshis_now` is the order's `last_satoshis` (`views.py:324` overwrites `:304-309`), not the sats of the take amount: the bond is checked against the public order (§ 3.2), never against that field. Invoice expiry = `t_to_expire(TAK)`; CLTV = (24 h + `escrow_duration`) × 1.7 / 8 min per block (`logics.py:1229-1249`, `.env-sample:161-163`): (86,400 s + 10,799 s) × 1.7 / 480 s ≈ 344 blocks with defaults, i.e. the longest a stuck bond can stay locked is days, not hours. Description (`:1405-1412`): « {node alias} - Taking 'Order {id}' Buying|Selling BTC for {amount}{currency} - Taker bond - This payment WILL FREEZE IN YOUR WALLET, check on the trading platform if the lock was successful. It will be unlocked (fail) unless you cheat or cancel unilaterally. » (stealth robots: « Payment reference: {uuid} … »).
6. When the bond locks: `finalize_contract` (`logics.py:1320-1380`): the order gets its taker, the range amount, `last_satoshis` frozen (**the trade amount in sats is final at this moment**: market moves before it change the amount, `:1332-1335`), status 6, `expires_at = now + escrow_duration`.
7. From 6, the coordinator answers each GET by role (`views.py:398-428`): the **seller** gets `escrow_invoice` and `escrow_satoshis` (generated once, `logics.py:1484-1551`; amount = `last_satoshis` + seller's fee share, `:733-749`; description « … Escrow amount for '{order}' - It WILL FREEZE IN YOUR WALLET. It will be released to the buyer once you confirm you received the fiat. It will automatically return if buyer does not confirm the payment. »); the **buyer** gets `invoice_amount` (= `last_satoshis` − buyer's fee share, `:650-669`) and the on-chain option `swap_allowed`, `swap_failure_reason`, `suggested_mining_fee_rate`, `swap_fee_rate` (`:671-730`). Fees: `FEE` 0.2 %, maker share `MAKER_FEE_SPLIT` 0.125 (`.env-sample:107-110`): **the taker pays 0.175 % of the trade**, the maker 0.025 %. Both see `trade_satoshis` once both bonds are locked, in the statuses listed at `views.py:346-356`, a list that leaves out 9 (« Sending fiat - In chatroom »): at 9 the answer has no `trade_satoshis` (`views.py:345-371`).
8. Escrow locked: 6 → 8, or 7 → 9 (`logics.py:1469-1481`). Payout submitted: 8 → 9, 6 → 7 or, if the escrow is already locked, 6 → 9 (`:909-947`). Entering 9 restarts the timer at 24 h.

### 1.3 Every action of `POST /api/order/` a taker uses

`api/views.py:536-695`; serializer `UpdateOrderSerializer` (`api/serializers.py`). After a successful action the answer is the same as a GET (`:695`), except a cancel that ends the order (4 or 12, `:604-615`): that one is an HTTP 200 whose body holds only `id`, `status`, `type`, `expires_at` and a `bad_request` key « This order has been cancelled », so a client that reads every `bad_request` as an error misreads it (the reference merges the body into its order, `Order.update`). A body the serializer refuses gets an empty 400 with no error code (`:539-540`), and an unknown order id on a POST is a 500 (`Order.objects.get`, `:542`).

| Action | Body | Allowed when | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `take` | `amount` (always, 0 for a fixed order), `password` (SHA-256 hex, private order) | 1 | § 1.2 |
| `cancel` | `cancel_status` (optional guard: refused with 1020 if the status moved, `logics.py:1011-1015`) | see § 1.4 | |
| `update_invoice` | `invoice` = **PGP clear-signed** bolt11, `routing_budget_ppm` (default 0) | buyer, both bonds locked, status 6 or 8, or 15 with the previous payout expired (`logics.py:835-907`) | signature checked against the robot's stored public key with gnupg; the signed text must use `Hash: SHA512` (the server splits on the literal header `-----BEGIN PGP SIGNED MESSAGE-----\nHash: SHA512\n\n`, `api/utils.py:628-648`); bad signature → 1048. The invoice must carry an amount **exactly** `invoice_amount − routing budget` (`api/lightning/lnd.py:451-539`), not be expired (the coordinator refuses only an invoice already expired, never one about to expire), its route hints payable within the budget; the same payment hash twice → 3002. The node's refusals carry **no error code**, only a `bad_invoice` text (`lnd.py:463-533`); 3001 means « not now » (status, a payout in flight, a failed payout not expired), not a bad invoice; 1047 (HTTP 403, `views.py:617-619`) answers any robot that is neither maker nor taker, a pre-taker included |
| `update_address` | `address` = PGP clear-signed address, `mining_fee_rate` (sat/vB, optional) | buyer, both bonds locked, status 6 or 8 (`logics.py:752-832`) | address checked by the coordinator's bitcoind, whose refusals carry **no error code**, only a `bad_address` text (`api/utils.py:106-121`; 4004/4005 exist but are not sent); fee ≥ max(2, economy rate) (4001) and ≤ 500 (4002); sent = amount − swap fee − fee rate × 280 vB, must be ≥ 20,000 sats (4003). Without `mining_fee_rate` the coordinator uses its own suggestion unchecked, up to 1,000 sat/vB (`logics.py:630-634`, `:802-803`), so Troc always sends a rate, bounded 2-500; the suggestion itself may exceed 500. `update_address` never checks `swap_allowed`: when no swap is offered, `order.payout_tx` is `None` and the action fails with a `500` (`logics.py:800-803`), not a refusal |
| `confirm` | — | 9 or 10 (`logics.py:1686-1735`) | buyer: « fiat sent », 9 → 10. Seller: only after « fiat sent » (1027); settles the escrow, returns both bonds, pays the buyer (13, or 14 at once for on-chain) |
| `undo_confirm` | — | buyer, 10 → 9 (`:1737-1756`) | |
| `dispute` | — | 9 or 10, and **not earlier than 6 h into the chat** (`expires_at − 18 h`, error 1054, `logics.py:497-501`) | settles escrow and both bonds to the coordinator at once (`:503-506`) |
| `submit_statement` | `statement`, 100 to 50,000 characters (`:536-569`) | 11 | both statements in → 16 |
| `rate_platform` | `rating` 1-5 | any time, overwrites (`:1785-1789`) | `rate_user` is not in the serializer's choices (`api/serializers.py:663-677`): refused with HTTP 400, the view's branch (`views.py:674-677`) is dead. Peer rating does not exist |

Rewards: `POST /api/reward/` with a PGP-signed invoice for the robot's `earned_rewards` (`views.py:1077-1108`, `logics.py:1886-`): a taker earns rewards when the maker loses its bond (§ 1.4) or wins a dispute (`api/AGENTS.md` « Dispute custody model »).

### 1.4 Cancelling, and when the bond is lost (`Logics.cancel_order`, `api/logics.py:1005-1181`)

| When (taker) | What a cancel does | Bond |
|---|---|---|
| 3, bond not yet paid | the pre-take is expired and its invoice cancelled (`:1061-1075`, `kick_taker` `:470-483`) | nothing paid; **the robot gets a penalty of `PENALTY_TIMEOUT`** (60 s in `.env-sample:141`) during which it cannot take (1012) |
| 3, timer runs out | same, without penalty (`clean_orders.py:89-97`) | nothing paid |
| 6 or 7 (bond locked, escrow not yet locked) | **taker bond settled (lost)**, order back to public, the maker credited with half of it as rewards (`:1108-1130`, `SLASHED_BOND_REWARD_SPLIT` 0.5) | **lost** |
| 8 or 9 (escrow locked) | **collaborative cancel only**: the first asks (`taker_asked_cancel`, `:1161-1168`), the GET then shows the peer `pending_cancel` (`views.py:439-449`); when the other side asks too, everything is returned: both bonds and the escrow, status 12 (`:1138-1159`, `:1183-1202`). The reference client offers it only in the chat (9): at 8 neither side has a button (`CancelButton.tsx:27-29`, `Prompts/PayoutWait.tsx`, `Payout.tsx`) | returned (free) |
| 10 | refused (1021): from « fiat sent » on, the only ways out are the seller's confirmation or a dispute | — |
| 11 and later, 4, 5, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18 | refused (1021, `:1019-1032`) | — |

When the **maker** cancels at 6 or 7, its bond is settled, the taker's returned, the taker rewarded with half of the maker's bond (`:1083-1106`). Collaborative cancel is free by design and stays so (`api/AGENTS.md` « Product intent »).

Bond outcomes on timeouts: § 1.1. In short, for a taker: **the bond is lost only by cancelling alone after it locked and before the escrow (6, 7), by letting one's own step time out (seller who does not lock the escrow; buyer who does not send a payout), or by losing a dispute; it is returned by the end of a successful trade (`confirm_fiat` `:1721-1726`), a collaborative cancel, a maker who cancels or times out, and a late bond on an order someone else took first.**

### 1.5 Disputes, payout failures, success (backend)

- Dispute (11): opened by either side from 6 h into the chat, or automatically when the 24 h chat timer ends. All funds go to the coordinator at once; the winner is paid through `earned_rewards` and `POST /api/reward/` with a new invoice (Lightning only, no on-chain path), the loser forfeits; 16 once both statements are in; 17/18 on the coordinator's decision (admin, `api/admin.py`). Statements 100-50,000 characters. Phase 4 for filing; phase 2 shows it (§ 2).
- Payout (13 → 14 or 15): Lightning payouts are retried by the follower (`follow_invoices.py`), `RETRY_TIME` 1 min (`.env-sample:143`); in 15 the buyer sees `retries`, `next_retry_time`, `failure_reason`, and once the invoice expired `invoice_expired` and `trade_satoshis`: he must send a new invoice (`update_invoice` on 15). On-chain payouts are queued (14 at once, `logics.py:1653-1668`) and the GET of a successful buyer gives `txid` or `tx_queued` and `address`, `sent_satoshis` (`views.py:511-526`).
- A buyer in 13 or 15 cannot take or make another order with that robot (1003).

### 1.6 Chat (backend)

- `GET /api/chat/?order_id=N&offset=K` → `{peer_connected, messages: [{index, time, message, nick}], peer_pubkey}`, messages with `index > offset`; allowed for maker and taker in 9, 10, 11, 16 (`chat/views.py` `get`). `POST /api/chat/` with `{order_id, PGP_message, offset?}` (`PostMessageSerializer`), only in 9 or 10; the server numbers it `count + 1` and answers the new messages when `offset` is given (`chat/views.py` `post`); that numbering has no lock and no unique constraint, so two posts at once share an index and the next one skips an index, and the query has no order (`chat/views.py:64`, `:158-166`, `chat/models.py:56-58`). `PGP_message` ≤ 5,000 characters is declared on the model (`chat/models.py:87`) but enforced nowhere on the API path: the serializer declares the field again without it (`chat/serializers.py:62-65`) and the column is a PostgreSQL `text` (found in #92). The peer's « connected » flag is its `last_login` less than a minute ago (`chat/views.py:78-91`), which the middleware refreshes only every 2 minutes (`robosats/middleware.py:95-96`): an active peer can show as not connected. Chats are deleted about 3 days after the order ends (`chat/tasks.py:26-31`).
- **The peer's public key comes from the coordinator** (`peer_pubkey`, the robot's stored `public_key`): a coordinator can substitute it (§ 4).
- The reference client now prefers a Nostr chat (NIP-17 gift-wrapped DMs) for Garage-Key robots when both have Nostr keys and the connection setting is « nostr », and falls back to this API chat otherwise (`chat/AGENTS.md` « Frontend Chat Implementations »); the WebSocket chat was removed from the frontend. Troc sends nothing to Nostr (phase 1 decision): it uses the API chat, which the reference still offers for `connection === 'api'`.

## 2. What phase 2 delivers, slice by slice

Principle: each slice ends with something the owner can do on a phone, and each leaves `main` coherent (nothing half-wired is reachable). Every slice reuses the screens of the reference (§ 1.7) in Bull's look; every deviation is listed in the slice.

### 2.0 Decisive facts for the order of the slices

1. **After the taker bond locks, a taker cannot leave alone for free** (§ 1.4): cancelling at 6 or 7 loses the bond; the free way out is the collaborative cancel, which the backend accepts at 8 and 9 but the reference client offers only in the chat (9) (`Prompts/Chat.tsx`, `CancelButton.tsx:27-29`). So a trial that pays a bond and wants it back free must reach the chat, i.e. needs the payout slice, and the peer (the owner's own decoy in the official client) must lock the escrow: at least the coordinator's minimum order (20,000 sats by default, `api/logics.py:31`) plus fees.
2. **The cheapest trial with a paid bond** is therefore: Troc takes the owner's decoy **sell** order (Troc buys), pays the bond, sees status 6, then cancels in Troc: the taker bond is lost, half of it is credited to the maker robot as rewards (`logics.py:1108-1130`, `SLASHED_BOND_REWARD_SPLIT` 0.5), which is the owner's own robot in the official client, where « Claim Rewards » can withdraw it (if the wallet used can receive such a small Lightning amount); the order goes back to public and the owner cancels it as maker for free (`logics.py:1048-1060`). Net cost ≈ half of a 2 % bond on a 20,000-sat order, about 200 sats, plus the routing or swap fees of his wallet. Cancelling at 3 before paying is free (a 60 s penalty on the robot, `.env-sample:141`).
3. **Chat interoperability** (§ 1.7, end of « 9 and 10 »): the official client shows by default only Nostr chat messages; a Troc that posts only to `/api/chat/` is unreadable by such a peer. The chat slice must decide this (§ 3.6, owner decision 1).
4. **On-chain payout is often impossible for small trades**: it exists only where the coordinator enabled swaps (`DISABLE_ONCHAIN` defaults to true in code, `logics.py:696`), within its `MIN_SWAP_AMOUNT`/`MAX_SWAP_AMOUNT`, and the sats sent after the swap fee and 280 vB of mining fee must be at least 20,000 (`logics.py:807-820`): a 20,000-sat trade can never be paid on-chain. The order's GET says it (`swap_allowed`, `swap_failure_reason`).
5. **The wallet's Lightning invoices expire early.** Bull receives Lightning only through its swap service, into the Liquid wallet, with an invoice that expires around the service's `confirmationDeadline` (`lib/features/receive/domain/usecases/create_receive_order_swap_usecase.dart:30,42,106-116`); RoboSats pays the buyer only at the end of the trade, possibly a day later; an expired invoice leads to status 15 and a new invoice (`logics.py:857-859`, `views.py:483-488`). RoboSats checks only that the invoice is not expired when submitted (`api/lightning/lnd.py:529-533`).

## 3. Architecture

Everything stays inside `features/p2p` and `packages/robosats`, with the AGENTS rules of the Troc section (use cases internal to the feature, failures as sealed types without fields, no robot type exported from `p2p.dart`, every robot request through `RobotRequestLimiter`, nothing printed or logged, texts in the feature's ARB files).

### 3.1 Package: the participant's order and the actions (`packages/robosats`)

- `CoordinatorOrder` (new, `lib/src/coordinator/coordinator_order.dart`): parsed from `GET /api/order/` with `JsonFields` and the read bounds already used for `PublicOrder` (`public_order.dart:90-135`); fields of § 1.2-1.5: `id`, `status` (0-18, anything else is a failure), `expiresAt`, `totalSecsExp`, `type`, `currency`, `amount` (the taken amount for a range), `hasRange`/`min`/`max`, `isExplicit`, `premium`, `bondSize`, `escrowDuration`, `isMaker`, `isTaker`, `isParticipant`, `isBuyer`, `isSeller`, `makerNick`, `makerHashId`, `takerNick`, `takerHashId`, `makerStatus`, `takerStatus`, `satoshisNow`, `tradeSatoshis`, `makerLocked`, `takerLocked`, `escrowLocked`, `bondInvoice`, `bondSatoshis`, `escrowInvoice`, `escrowSatoshis`, `invoiceAmount`, `swapAllowed`, `swapFailureReason`, `suggestedMiningFeeRate`, `swapFeeRate`, `isFiatSent`, `isDisputed`, `pendingCancel`, `askedForCancel`, `chatLastIndex`, `statementSubmitted`, `retries`, `nextRetryTime`, `failureReason`, `invoiceExpired`, `expiryReason`, `penalty`, the summary fields (`views.py:500-504`, `Logics.summarize_trade`), `txid`/`txQueued`/`address`/`sentSatoshis`. Each field is read with the JSON type the view writes for that robot: the serializer's decimals are strings (`api/serializers.py:83-107`), but a raw `Decimal` the view adds is written by Django REST framework's encoder as a number (a participant's coordinates, `views.py:320-321`; `premium_now`; the summary's amounts and percentages; `routing_budget_sats`, `logics.py:2128`), as the reference's schema says (`docs/assets/schemas/api-latest.yaml`, `OrderDetail`). The maker's free text (description, payment method) goes through `displayText`, as today; the coordinator's own labels are read as enums and never kept (`swapFailureReason` as `SwapRefusal`, `failureReason` as `PayoutFailureReason`, `expiryReason` as `OrderExpiryReason`); the nicknames are not exposed but derived from the hash ids, and an answer whose nickname differs is refused. Only this robot's own summary is read (`maker_summary` or `taker_summary`, and `platform_summary`), without the payout's payment hash and preimage, and an invalid summary is dropped alone (display only), never the order with it. The parser takes the robot's identity and refuses an answer whose roles contradict each other or that robot: maker and taker both; `is_participant` not maker or taker; this robot's hash id as maker or taker when the answer says otherwise; buyer or seller not what the order's type and the role give; `ur_nick` not this robot's nickname; status 3 for anyone but an unnamed pre-taker with its bond; a taker not named past a pre-take, or from status 6; a robot that is no participant at any status but 1, or given a participant's field; a bond invoice outside 0 (maker) or 3 (pre-taker), an escrow invoice outside a seller at 6 or 7, `invoice_amount` to a seller. Invoices are bounded (2,000 visible ASCII characters) and kept as opaque strings; the package does not trust them (the feature decodes them).
- Client members (shown here by their order arguments; each also takes the coordinator and the network first and the robot's identity, as `fetchKnownRobot` does, but the cancel, and is sent on the robot's own session with the short header). Deviation: the reference reads the order with the full first-contact header, keys included (`frontend/src/models/Order.model.ts:264-273`, `Robot.model.ts:72-86`, `services/api/ApiWebClient/index.ts:16-29`); Troc sends the short one, since the coordinator ignores the keys of a token it knows (`robosats/middleware.py:62-178`) and a read must not create a robot the coordinator does not know (it answers `RobotUnknown`, 7001). The members: `fetchOrder(orderId)`, `takeOrder(orderId, {amount, password})`, `cancelOrder({identity, lastRead})` (the coordinator, the network, the order and the guard `cancel_status` all come from the last read of the order, a `CoordinatorOrder` read by that robot; the guard is the stored status, 1 for a pre-take shown as 3, and always sent; a last read by another robot is refused before anything is sent), `submitPayoutInvoice({identity, lastRead, invoice: SignedPayoutInvoice, routingBudget: RoutingBudgetPpm})` and `submitPayoutAddress({identity, lastRead, address: SignedPayoutAddress, miningFeeRate: MiningFeeRate})` (as the cancel, from the last read; the signed values are made only by the robot's unlocked key, the budget 0-99,998 ppm and the rate 2-500 sat/vB with 3 decimals; both return `Result<PayoutOutcome, OrderActionFailure>`, #89), `confirmFiat`, `undoConfirmFiat`, `openDispute`, `ratePlatform(orderId, rating)`, `fetchChat(orderId, offset)`, `postChat(orderId, pgpMessage, offset)`. `fetchOrder` returns `Result<CoordinatorOrder, CoordinatorFailure>`; `takeOrder` returns `Result<TakeOrderOutcome, OrderActionFailure>`, the outcome `OrderTakeStarted` (the pre-take with its bond), `OrderAlreadyTakenByThisRobot` or `OrderNotTaken`; `cancelOrder` returns `Result<CancelOrderOutcome, OrderActionFailure>`, `OrderCancelEnded` or `OrderCancelAccepted`; an `OrderActionFailure` is `OrderActionRefused` (nothing was done) or `OrderActionOutcomeUnknown` (read the order again), each with its `CoordinatorFailure` as `cause` (§ 2.1, package). The later money members will follow the same shape. The decade-coded errors (`api/errors.py`: `bad_request`, `bad_invoice`, `bad_address`) become typed failures (`RobotHasActiveOrder` for 1000-1003, `OrderAmountOutOfRange`, `OrderTakePenalty(secondsLeft)` (the seconds exist only inside the English message of 1012, `api/errors.py:15`, so they are read from that exact message when it matches and left out otherwise; the GET carries `penalty`, a date, `views.py:264-267`), `OrderStatusChanged`, `OrderNotCancellable`, `CoordinatorNodeUnavailable`, `OrderTakeExpired`, `OrderEscrowExpired`, `OrderNotFound`, `OrderCancelled`, `OrderNotAvailable`, `OrderWrongPassword`, `OrderNotPublic`, `OrderReadElsewhere`, `OrderRequestRefused(errorCode)`, and, for the payout, the sealed `PayoutRefused` kinds: `PayoutInvoiceRefused`, `PayoutAddressRefused`, `PayoutSignatureRefused`, `PayoutNotNow`, …, #89) with no coordinator text carried (the screen has its own texts; a coordinator's free text is dropped, as phase 1 does for robots); error codes are read on `4xx` answers only.
- A POST is never retried by the transport (only a `GET` is, on connection failure: `tor_coordinator_transport.dart:103`) and never by the use cases after a timeout: the action may have happened; the next poll tells.
- Bodies: JSON with `Content-Type: application/json`, as the reference sends them (`services/api/ApiWebClient/index.ts:13,57-79`); numbers as the reference sends them (`amount` a number, in sats divided by 10⁸ for an order in BTC, § 1.2; `rating` a number the serializer's string choices accept; `cancel_status` a number, the stored status, § 2.1).

### 3.2 Domain: the trade and its state machine

- `Trade` (new): network, coordinator (from the federation list, never from the answer), order id, robot (index of a key, as a `NetworkRobot` derived on demand, never held in a state), role (buyer/seller, fixed at the take from the public order's type: taking a sell order makes a buyer), the public order as seen when taken (amounts, premium, bond size, escrow duration), and the last `CoordinatorOrder` read.
- `TradeStatus` (#86, `features/p2p/lib/src/domain/trade/`): a sealed type, one class per status the taker can see (`WaitingForTakerBond(bond, deadline)`, `WaitingForBoth`, `WaitingForEscrow`, `WaitingForPayout` (each with the role, the seller's escrow invoice, the buyer's payout amount), `Chatting(fiatSent, askedToCancel, peerAskedToCancel)`, `InDispute(statementSubmitted)`, `SendingSats`, `RoutingFailed(retries, next, reason, invoiceExpired)`, `Succeeded(summary)`, `WaitingDisputeResolution`, `DisputeWon`, `DisputeLost`, `Expired(reason)`, `Cancelled`, `TakenByAnother(bondMayHaveBeenLost)`, and the ones this note missed: `PreTakeEnded(bondMayHaveBeenLost)` (public again after the pre-take), `BondForfeited` (public again or 1044 after the bond locked: every such path of the reference settles the taker's bond), `PreTakeUnavailable` (error 1044 during the pre-take: not an end, since a maker who pauses the order gives its pre-taker 1044, `api/views.py:269-275`, and a pause does not expire pre-takes, `api/logics.py:1758-1783`; it becomes `TakenByAnother` once the pre-take's deadline and a minute for the coordinator's loops have passed), `StatusUnknown` (no answer understood yet on the trail)), built by one pure function `TradeStatus.from(previous, answer, terms, now, reader)` that the property tests drive (§ 5). `previous` is never absent: a trade starts from the take's own answer (`TradeStatus.afterTake(OrderTakeStarted, …)`), a resumed trade from its stored trail (§ 3.5), a take whose answer was lost from `TradeTrail.forPendingTake`, a recovered one from `TradeTrail.recovered(…)`, on which the wallet never pays. The terms are refused when the coordinator announces a taker fee above 1 % (`TakerFeeTooHigh`; the reference's default is 0.175 %, the highest recorded 0.2625 %). `bondMayHaveBeenLost` (on `PreTakeEnded`, `TakenByAnother` and `Cancelled`) is false right after the take, and after the pre-take only when it was last seen less than the escrow duration before; it is true for `Cancelled` after 6, 7 or 8 (the order may have gone back to the book with the bond settled, then been cancelled by its maker): a bond that locked unseen while the app was closed can be lost by a step left undone, and then « Nothing was taken from you » would be false. The UI maps each class to a panel; no rule in the cubit.
- **Checked, not trusted** (each failed check is a typed `TradeWarning` on the status, never an automatic action; every warning forbids « Pay with this wallet »; its effect on the QR code is one of: shown, hidden until « Show anyway », hidden):
  - role: the parser of `packages/robosats` already checks that the roles agree with each other, with the order's type and with the robot that asked (`CoordinatorOrder`); the state machine refuses an answer that makes this robot the maker or the other side than the one it took (`TradeRoleMismatch`), or that is about another coordinator, network or order (`TradeOrderMismatch`): « Do not pay anything. ».
  - status moves (#86, from the reference, each with its lines in `TradeEdges.steps`): 3→6, 3→public again, 3→1044 (the order paused, another taker's bond locked first, or the order expired: not an end while the pre-take may still live, see `PreTakeUnavailable`), 3→1043; from that 1044: back to 3 (same invoice only), 6, public again, 1043, or 1044 for good; 6→7, 6→8, 6→9, 6→5 (both bonds settled), 6→public again (this taker cancelled: never a timeout), 6→1043 (the maker cancelled); 7→9, 7→5 (a taker buyer whose maker did not lock the escrow, or for both roles the cleaner's expiry when a hold invoice cannot be found, `clean_orders.py:72-81`, which also gives 8→5, 9→5 and 10→5), 7→public again (a cancel, or a taker seller's timeout), 7→1043; 8→9, 8→5, 8→public again (taker buyer only: no payout in time), 8→1043 (collaborative); 9→10, 9→11, 9→1043; 10→9, 10→11, 10→13, 10→14; 11→16, 11→17, 11→18, and, missed by this note, 11→1043 and 11→13 (the coordinator's « return everything » and « successful trade », `api/admin.py:195-350`), the same from 16; 13→14, 13→15, 15→13, 15→14; public again→1044 or 1043. Several moves happen between two reads (a poll every 3 to 300 s, nothing while the app is closed), so any path of these is allowed. Any other edge: to a status that goes on, the status with `TradeStatusUnexpected` (« Unexpected change on the coordinator's side. », polling goes on); to an end, or back to 3 after it, or to a status a taker is never shown (0, 2, 4 or 12 in an order, public with this robot as a participant), the answer is refused (`TradeStatusUnexpected`, `TradeStatusRegressed`) and the last status stays. After any such breach every later status carries `CoordinatorInconsistent`. A terminal status is never left.
  - what the wallet may pay: at most the amount the user saw and accepted in the take confirmation (the bond) or in the escrow panel's confirmation (`walletPaymentFor`); a larger invoice is refused, not offered with an override.
  - amounts (`TradeTolerances`, each with its reason): the bond invoice's amount equals `bond_satoshis`; `bond_satoshis` within ±2 % (+1 sat for the truncation) of `bond_size % ×` the sats of the amount taken at the book's price (the book's largest amount over its `satoshis_now`, as `TakerEstimate`; the coordinator fixes the bond at its own price at the first read of the pre-take, `logics.py:1402-1404`); from 6 on, the answer's `satoshis_now` (the order's `last_satoshis`, frozen when the bond locked, `logics.py:1332-1335`) within ±2 % of those sats (this is the implied price check; at 3 that field is stale and not read); `bond_size`, premium, currency, amount (the one taken for a range), sats of an order priced in sats and escrow duration equal to the public order's (`TradeTermsChanged`); the escrow invoice's amount equals `escrow_satoshis` equals `trade_satoshis`; the buyer's `invoice_amount` **equals** `trade_satoshis` (both are `payout_amount`, `views.py:367-371`, `:413-428`: a smaller one would cost the buyer); the taker fee implied (`escrow − last_satoshis` or `last_satoshis − invoice_amount`) between 0 and `last_satoshis ×` the `taker_fee` of `/api/info/` plus 1 sat. Both ways for the 2 %: a figure far from the order's, even in the user's favour, says the coordinator computed with other terms. A failed amount check hides the QR and the copy behind « The amount asked is not what the order says. » with « Show anyway » (deviation: the reference shows what it gets).
  - invoices: § 4.4.
  - expiry: the countdown uses the device clock against `expires_at`; a coordinator that moves `expires_at` back within a status is shown as is (it decides), with `DeadlineMovedBack`.
- What cannot be checked and is said in the take confirmation and README: the coordinator holds the bond, the escrow and the decision of disputes; it can settle a bond unfairly or keep the escrow. One robot per coordinator limits this to that coordinator (§ 4.1).

### 3.3 What the phase needs from the robot layer (#79, #81)

An interface in the domain, implemented by whatever #79/#81 decide (or by the interim of § 2.1):

- `Future<Result<NetworkRobot, P2pFailure>> robotForNewTrade(RoboSatsNetwork, Coordinator)`: a robot that never held an order (no binding with a recorded trade, no `last_order_id` on any read), of a key the user may trade with (generated here or derived from the seed; never a pasted key's robot, per #79), **bound to that coordinator atomically before it is returned**, and recorded as reserved so that a second take cannot get it; with RoboSats' rule « one robot, one active order » (`logics.py:75-135`) and its advice of one robot per trade, a robot is never reused for a second trade by Troc.
- `Future<Result<NetworkRobot, P2pFailure>> robotOfTrade(TradeRecord)`: the robot of a recorded trade (key fingerprint, index, network), refusing if the key is not in the vault any more (`TradeKeyMissing`: « The Garage Key of this trade is not on this device. Paste it back to finish the trade. »).
- `Stream<List<TradeRecord>> activeTrades(RoboSatsNetwork)` (in #85 a stream of `Result<List<TradeRecord>, P2pFailure>`, so that a database that cannot be read gives a typed failure, not a list) and, for recovery, `Future<List<FoundTrade>> findTradesOfKey(NetworkGarageKey)` (read every bound robot back, take its `active_order_id`; for a pasted key, #79's search on every coordinator).
- #85 implements the interim (`InterimTradeRobots`): the robot is bound to the coordinator by one statement that also checks that its index has no binding and no trade and that the key is recorded as drawn here on that network and as nothing else on any network, and says whether this call wrote it; the binding is the reservation, so a robot bound and never recorded (the app killed in between) stays bound, without a trade, and is never returned again. `findTradesOfKey` is left to #79, since it needs coordinator reads.
- The binding's provenance stays the rule for pasted robots: an `acknowledged` robot may finish an order it already holds (found by recovery) but is never returned by `robotForNewTrade` (answers an open point that the design of the robot panel, #29, left to phase 2).
- Each trade record carries its origin (§ 3.5), as a fact about the trade: `takenHere`, written only by `TakeOrderUsecase` when this device takes the order, or `recovered`, written only when the user explicitly takes over a trade that `findTradesOfKey` found. What uses it: the robot of a `recovered` trade may finish the order it holds but never starts a new one (#79, owner decision 8), and the trade screens can say where a trade came from. It is no gate: there is no approval gate (§ 3.7).

### 3.4 The robot's key pair during a trade

Signing a payout and the chat need the robot's unlocked key. The pair is not stored (as in phase 1): the trade use cases read the robot back (`ReadRobotOnCoordinatorUsecase`, proven pair) once per trade screen session, unlock it with the token in the compute isolate (32 MiB of SHA-256, `RobotKeyComputePort`), and keep the `UnlockedRobotPgpKey` in a `TradeKeyring` owned by the trade's use cases, dropped when the trade screen closes or the app goes to the background (lifecycle `hidden`/`paused`), re-unlocked on return. Recommendation over « one S2K per action »: the chat decrypts every 5 s and a proof per message would cost seconds of CPU; the keyring holds the same secret the token already derives at any time, for no longer than the screen. A peer key or a robot pair that changes between two reads of one trade is refused (`TradeKeyChanged`).

### 3.5 Stored on the device (`p2p.sqlite` schema 3)

One new table, `trades`, written before the take's POST as a « pending take » (§ 4.5, § 7 decision 2), so that a lost answer is recoverable, and never deleted by the app; one row per robot, ever (primary key: key fingerprint and robot index), so that the database itself refuses a second trade of a robot (#85):

| Column | Why |
|---|---|
| `network`, `key_fingerprint`, `robot_index` | which robot (same keys as `robot_bindings`; foreign key to it) |
| `coordinator_alias`, `coordinator_onion` | which coordinator, checked against the binding at each read (a changed coordinator is refused, as for robots) |
| `order_id` | which order |
| `role` (`buyer`/`seller`) | fixed at the take |
| `origin` (`takenHere`/`recovered`) | whether this device took the trade or the user took over a trade found by recovery (§ 3.3) |
| `taken_at` | when the record was written: ordering |
| `confirmed_at` (nullable) | set once, when the coordinator's answer or a later read shows the robot as the taker; absent, with `closed_at` absent too, the record is a pending take; a `recovered` record has it from the start (decided in #85: two times set once each rather than a status column, which would repeat `closed_at` and allow states the statements never make) |
| `closed_at` (nullable) | set once, when a terminal status is read (4, 5, 12, 14, 17, 18, or taken by another), or when a pending take turns out not to have happened (§ 4.5), so that active trades are those without it; a closed record never changes |

**Added by #86 (the trade's state machine), for #87 to store:** the trade's terms (`TradeTermsRecord`: the public order as read just before the take, the amount taken, the coordinator's `taker_fee`, every field plain and versioned), written with the pending take and **set once** (never written again), and the trade's trail (`TradeTrailRecord`: the trade it belongs to (order, coordinator, network, role), the last position, the payment hash of the first bond invoice and of the first escrow invoice, the last deadline, the pre-take's last read and deadline, the breach flag, whether the history is unknown), written after each read, only when `TradeTrailRecord.mayReplace` allows it over the stored record (a breach and an unknown history stay, pinned hashes stay set and equal, the read never goes back, the pre-take's last read never moves back, the position moves only along the edges, or with a breach, and never out of an end) and never from a read older than the stored one. #87's schema step adds database triggers that refuse any update of the terms (as `trades_set_once` does for `trades`, #102) and any write of a trail that clears a breach, changes whether the history is known, or clears or changes a pinned hash. Without them a restart would forget the pinned hashes and the breach, and a hostile coordinator could have a second escrow paid after any restart (review of #106). A recorded trade is resumed only from them (`TradeTerms.restore`, `TradeTrail.restore(record, terms:, now:)`, which refuse a malformed record, a trail of another trade, a read after the resume beyond a clock step, and a coordinator no longer in the list, each with its own type), never from a fresh trail; a trade whose coordinator left the list (`TradeCoordinatorGone`) is shown read-only, never restarted; a trade found by recovery gets `TradeTrail.recovered`, on which the wallet never pays. A payment started is recorded per step and per attempt (« payment started for hash H of step S »: a seller pays a bond and an escrow), in the `trade_payments` table below.

Payments from this wallet (« Pay with this wallet ») are not in schema 3: #85 first added three nullable columns for them, then dropped them after its security review, since one hash set once can represent neither a retry after a failed payment (§ 2.1) nor a seller who pays the bond and then the escrow (§ 2.6), and the database did not keep such a column from being cleared. The pull request that adds the payment adds a table `trade_payments`, in its own schema version: one row per attempt, a foreign key to `trades`, the purpose (bond or escrow), the payment hash (unique), the start time, the wallet's swap id and the outcome, with database-level rules that set each once. #85 also made the database keep the trades' own rules: a foreign key to the binding on the trade's network, and triggers that refuse a delete, any change but setting `confirmed_at` or `closed_at` once, and a second insert of a robot (`INSERT OR REPLACE` included).

Not stored: invoices (only the pinned payment hashes of the trail, and the payments of the later table above), the coordinator's answers and the amounts they give (the terms hold the public order's amounts; everything else is read again from the coordinator), the chat, the peer's key or nick, the robot's pair; the last status is not stored either, only the trail's last position, from which the state machine resumes (a terminal status is also kept as `closed_at`). Recovery after a reinstall or a lost file: § 3.3 `findTradesOfKey`. The chat is kept in memory and read again from index 0 at each session (the coordinator keeps it until about 3 days after the end, `chat/tasks.py:26-31`); the export is the user's copy. Migration 2 → 3 with the drift snapshot and steps as for schema 2 (`AGENTS.md` « One robot, one coordinator »).

### 3.6 The chat

- Format (§ 1.6-1.7): OpenPGP message encrypted to self and peer, signed, armored, line breaks sent as `\`; `POST /api/chat/` `{PGP_message, order_id, offset}`; read with `GET /api/chat/?order_id&offset`. Polling every 5 s while the chat is shown (the reference's `CHAT_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`), every 25 s while the trade screen is open on another status; never in the background in phase 2. The transport has no websocket, and the reference itself dropped its websocket chat (`chat/AGENTS.md`): polling is the reference's way.
- Ordering and replay: messages keyed by the coordinator's `index`; an index seen twice keeps the first decrypted message (the reference does the same, `EncryptedApiChat/index.tsx:174-178`); the reference's own numbering can repeat or skip an index (§ 1.6), so a gap or a repeat is shown, not taken as an attack; a message shown again at a new index leaves the indexes in order: the page's `repeatsACiphertext` (the same canonical armor twice) catches it under another armor, and only the decrypted message's `ChatSignatureKey` (signer, signature time, digest of the signed text), kept for the trade, catches it whatever the coordinator did to the armor or to the packet framing (#92); a message whose signature does not verify with the claimed sender's key is shown with « Signature not valid: this message may not come from {nick}. » (the reference only shows an icon); the coordinator chooses the order and can drop or replay messages: it cannot forge or read them unless it substitutes the peer's key.
- The peer's key: from `peer_pubkey`, parsed by the package (bounded), and **must be named after the peer's hash id that the order gives** (`RoboSats ID <hash id>`, the robot key's user ID, `RobotPgpKey.isNamedAfter`); pinned for the trade at its first read (`TradeKeyChanged` if it changes). A coordinator can still give a key of its own named after the peer (it knows the hash id): that is the residual risk of the reference too (§ 4.1). Given to both sides from the first page, such keys let it read, rewrite, encrypt again and sign again every message both ways, and every message verifies: only comparing both public keys outside the app catches it, which the reference's « Don't trust, verify » dialog allows (`frontend/src/components/Dialogs/AuditPGP.tsx:107-169`) and the chat panel offers too (#92, rules for #93 in `AGENTS.md`).
- Text: plain text only, cleaned by `displayText` (control characters, bidi overrides), at most 3,250 bytes sent (`OutgoingChatMessage.maxTextBytes`), at most `PgpLimits` read; links shown, never opened; never logged.
- **Interop with the official client (decision needed).** The official client sends each message to Nostr and to the coordinator, and by default displays only Nostr (§ 1.7). So a Troc that only posts to the API is read by Troc and by an official client set to « api », not by a default one. Options: (A, recommended) Troc sends each message also as NIP-17 kind-14 gift wraps (NIP-59, NIP-44 v2 encryption, BIP-340 signatures with the robot's Nostr key, which `NostrKeyPair` already derives) to the coordinator's relay `ws://<onion>/relay/` over the robot's Tor session, and keeps reading the API (the official peer posts there too); it adds a websocket to the transport and three primitives to review; it publishes nothing beyond what the official client publishes, to the same relay. (B) Troc posts to the API only and tells the user, in the chat, « If your peer does not answer, they may not see your messages: ask them, by any other means, to set RoboSats › Settings › Connection to API. » — honest but poor. (C) Troc stops sending a Nostr key at robot creation: the backend would then return the string `"None"` as the key (`views.py:279,317` `str(None)`), which the official client takes as a key (`EncryptedChat/index.tsx:349-354`): it would break its chat entirely; rejected. A first measurement in slice 3 (a Troc robot and the owner's official client on the decoy, both in the chat) confirms or refutes this reading before the Nostr work starts.

### 3.7 No approval gate (owner's decision, 2026-11-13)

An approval gate was designed and written (a constant that kept every mainnet trade closed until the owner approved real money), then dropped: the owner decided on 2026-11-13 that the builds he installs may trade on mainnet from the first one that can take an order, and that every real trial is his own. The P2P tab stays behind superuser and developer mode, as the rest of the tab. What stays is a rule on the developers and agents: they never take or cancel an order, never request or pay an invoice, never call a money member against a real coordinator, and stay on testnet for every authenticated call; automated tests do the same, and the live integration test stays read-only and testnet-only. The client's money members (`takeOrder`, `cancelOrder`, `submitPayoutInvoice`, `submitPayoutAddress`, `confirmFiat`, `undoConfirmFiat`, `openDispute`, and a reward claim when it comes) are named only in the trade use cases (`lib/src/domain/usecases/trade/<name>_usecase.dart`), so that every call that locks, moves or releases funds sits in one reviewed place; an architecture test (`trade_money_calls_architecture_test.dart`) pins it, and that the integration tests name no money member and keep their robots on testnet. What protects the user instead of a gate: the take confirmation states the bond at stake and says « Real bitcoin » in the warning colour on mainnet (Troc text, § 2.1); the checks of amounts, roles and statuses (§ 3.2) and of every invoice (§ 4.4) before any QR is shown or any invoice is paid with « Pay with this wallet »; the confirmation in the trade screen (amount, quoted fees, what stays in the wallet) before the wallet pays, and the wallet's PIN if the owner so decides (§ 7, owner decision 9); the PIN before « Confirm received », which releases the escrow (§ 2.5); and the « keep the app open » line on every timed panel (§ 3.8).

### 3.8 Polling, the limiter, background

- `WatchTradeUsecase` reads the order through `RobotRequestLimiter` (2 in flight per coordinator and network: `AGENTS.md` « Robot requests ») at the reference's cadence (`GarageContext.tsx:40-60`): 3 s at 3, 8 s at 6-8, 10 s at 9, 10, 13, 30 s at 15, 60 s at 14 then stop, 100 s at 11, 300 s at 16-18, stop at 4, 5, 12; ×5 when the trade screen is not the one shown but the app is in the foreground (reference: `:135`); **nothing in the background** in phase 2. The first read after the app returns to the foreground is immediate. A busy answer uses the limiter's backoff; a timeout or a transport failure waits for the next tick (no retry storm: at most one read in flight per trade); after three failed reads in a row the panel says « Cannot reach {coordinator}. Retrying… » and keeps the last known status with its age.
- Leaving the trade screen does not drop the trade: the list of active trades keeps the slow polling while the P2P tab is open; leaving the tab stops it (sessions stay up, as for the book).
- What the user loses when the app is closed: everything is on the coordinator's clock: a taker bond not paid in time (no loss), a payout not given before the end of `escrow_duration` (bond lost, `logics.py:445-458`), an escrow not locked in time as seller (bond lost, `:404-421`), a buyer who does not confirm « sent » and a seller who does not confirm « received » within 24 h (automatic dispute, all funds to the coordinator until it decides, `:460-468`, `:503-506`). The panels say it (§ 2.1) with the time; the « Active trades » line in the P2P tab shows the next deadline. Notifications are phase 4.

### 3.9 What the shell lends

`P2pFeature.route` already receives `reauthenticate` (`features/p2p/lib/public/p2p_facade.dart:57-69`). Phase 2 adds, through the same composition (`lib/locator.dart`): `P2pWalletPort` with `newReceiveAddress(network)` (from `GetReceiveAddressUsecase`, `lib/core/wallet/domain/usecases/get_receive_address_usecase.dart`), `newLightningInvoice(network, sats)` (from `CreateReceiveOrderSwapUsecase`, `lib/features/receive/domain/usecases/create_receive_order_swap_usecase.dart`) in slice 2, and, in slice 1 for the bond and slice 6 for the escrow, the Instant Payments wallet's available balance, a quote for paying a BOLT11 invoice (fees and limits), and `payInvoice(...)` (from `CreateSendSwapUsecase`, `lib/features/send/domain/usecases/create_send_swap_usecase.dart`), which creates, funds and follows the swap with the wallet's own code and reports its progress (sent, pending, failed, refunded) to the trade screen, without the wallet's send screens; whether those use cases can run without their screens, what they need from the shell, how fees and limits are quoted, and what happens to a swap whose hold invoice is cancelled are settled by a short study first (#100), and the port's payment is reviewed as money code; and the BOLT11 decoder the shell already depends on (`bolt11_decoder`, `pubspec.yaml:119-122`) behind a small domain port, so that `features/p2p` gets no new dependency on the wallet's core.

### 3.10 Failures and texts

Every new failure is a type without a field (sealed `P2pFailure`), mapped in `p2p_failure_l10n.dart`: the coordinator's text is never shown (it is coordinator-chosen and English only). Texts of the reference are taken from `en.json`/`fr.json` where they exist (§ 1.7), Troc's own French for the TradeBox titles the reference leaves untranslated, and Troc's own texts (marked « Troc ») for its deviations; the full list goes into the project's P2P text catalogue, with its conventions, in the first PR of each slice.

## 4. Security and privacy

### 4.1 Threat model for a taker

| Who | Can | Troc's answer |
|---|---|---|
| The order's coordinator | settle the bond or keep the escrow unfairly; lie about amounts, status, the peer's key; give an invoice of another node; drop, reorder or replay chat messages; act as the robot (it holds the token hash, the whole credential) | it is the custodian by design (RoboSats' trust model). Troc checks what can be checked (§ 3.2, § 4.4), warns before money moves, pins the peer key per trade, never acts automatically on a surprising answer, uses one robot per trade so that a bad coordinator affects one trade |
| A second coordinator replaying the robot's credential | act on the robot's order elsewhere, `confirm` included (`docs/spikes/robot-coordinator.md` « The shared token hash ») | prevented by the binding: the robot of a trade was never sent anywhere else (`robotForNewTrade` binds before the first contact); a pasted key's robots never start a trade (§ 3.3) |
| The peer | lie in the chat (scams: reversible payment methods, fake proofs), not pay, not confirm, open disputes | the reference's warnings (« Beware scams », reversible payments dialog already shown on the order details, the 80-day text), the dispute path; the peer cannot touch funds except through the coordinator |
| A network observer | see Tor traffic to coordinators | everything over the embedded Tor, `.onion` only, one Tor session per robot (`AGENTS.md`); nothing to Nostr unless option A of § 3.6, which also goes over Tor to the coordinator's onion |
| A stolen, unlocked phone | open the P2P tab and act on a running trade; with the app open, take their own order, pay the bond from this wallet, cancel after the bond locks and recover half of it as rewards on their own maker robot, again and again, draining the wallet | the wallet's PIN at app start as today; the PIN again before « Confirm received » (escrow release) on mainnet; whether the PIN is asked before « Pay with this wallet » is an open decision for the owner (§ 7, owner decision 9); no PIN for « fiat sent » (the reference has a dialog; a false « sent » risks the thief's victim's bond only after a dispute) |
| The payer wallet (swap service) | delay a refund, refuse long holds | « Pay with another wallet » (the QR and the copy) always offered, and opened when this wallet cannot pay; « Pay with this wallet », the main way, from slice 1 at the owner's request, inside the trade screen, after a confirmation that states the amount and the quoted fees, with a warning that carries what the first trials measure (#8) and the refund path that #100 establishes |

### 4.2 The PGP layer

What phase 2 relies on: `signCleartext` (payout), `encryptChatMessage`/`decryptChatMessage`, `PgpPublicKey.parse` of the peer's key, the proof and unlock of the robot's pair. Proven against GnuPG and OpenPGP.js, bounded against hostile input (`docs/spikes/pgp.md` § Interoperability matrix, § Bounds and rejections), never yet run against a coordinator for signatures or chat (`docs/spikes/pgp.md` l. 72, issue #7): slice 2's first real payout and slice 3's first real message are those checks. Still provisional: the hand-written layer is replaced by a Rust library before any release (#25); `pinenacl` is neither audited nor constant-time (`AGENTS.md`). Inherent property to keep in mind (`docs/spikes/pgp.md` l. 105): a chat signature is a valid cleartext signature of the same text, so the chat must refuse to send a text that is exactly a Lightning invoice or a Bitcoin address of the user's own payout (`SendChatMessageUsecase` checks the trimmed text against the payout submitted for this trade). Everything of phase 2 that changes `lib/src/pgp/` or adds a primitive (Nostr option A) gets the key-material review of the AGENTS section.

### 4.3 Never logged or stored, screens and clipboard

- Never logged, printed, put in a fixture or a report: the token, the token hash, an `Authorization` header, the unlocked key, chat plaintext and ciphertext, invoices, addresses, payment hashes, coordinator-chosen text (as phase 1 for robots); the print-capture tests of the robot use cases extend to the trade use cases.
- Never stored: § 3.5 (only the trade record).
- Screen capture: the chat panel holds the parties' payment details: protect the chat panel with `BullScreenPrivacyGate` as the Garage is (deviation for privacy); the invoice panels are not protected (a bond invoice is public, and the user may need a screenshot of it).
- Clipboard: copying an invoice is allowed (public); a pasted payout invoice or address is read once on the user's tap (as `GarageKeyField`), never polled; the clipboard is not cleared for invoices (not secret).
- Text from the coordinator or the peer is plain text, cleaned, bounded, never markup, links never opened (phase 1 rules).

### 4.4 The rule for showing or sending an invoice

Before showing a bond or escrow QR, decode the BOLT11 locally (bounded: at most 2,000 visible ASCII characters, then the decoder, whose every throw or odd result is a typed refusal) and show, under the QR: amount in sats, expiry time, network (`lnbc` on mainnet, `lntb` on testnet; `lntbs`, `lnbcrt` and any other prefix are another network and are refused: else the QR is not shown), and the description (the coordinator's text: « Taking 'Order {id}' Buying BTC … Taker bond … », `logics.py:1405-1412`, shown cleaned). Refuse to show it (with « Show anyway » for the amount cases only) when: the amount differs from what the order says (§ 3.2); the network differs (no override: the invoice is an `UnusableInvoice`, never a `CheckedInvoice`); the invoice has already expired; the payment hash is not the one of the first invoice of the same step in the trade (the backend returns the same invoice until it is paid, `logics.py:1393-1399`, `:1490-1495`; the first hash is pinned for the whole trade, stored with the trade's trail so that a restart does not forget it, and a changed hash is a breach of trust: the wallet never pays on that trade again). The app's decoder (`bolt11_decoder` at f5deec21, implemented in the shell behind the `Bolt11Decoder` port) **does not check the signature and does not recover the payee node key** (#86): the payee is compared with the coordinator's node keys of the federation list (`federation.dart`, the node keys pinned by `embedded_data_test.dart`) only when the invoice states it (`n`), and a mismatch is shown (owner-facing: « This invoice is not from {coordinator}'s known Lightning node. Do not pay it. »). **In practice the payee is never verified**: a coordinator's LND hold invoice states no `n` field, so the check never runs, and the invoice is trusted as the coordinator's own. Verifying the BOLT11 signature and recovering the payee key, to compare it with the federation's node keys, is a separate follow-up issue with a key-material review. The adapter (`lib/features/p2p_bolt11/`) also reads the amount again in the strict form of BOLT 11 (the decoder accepts `1.5u`, `1e3`, leading zeros) and bounds the expiry field, which the decoder wraps beyond 12 words. For a payout invoice: amount exactly the computed figure (§ 2.2), the trade's network, expiry at least the remaining `escrow_duration` + 24 h preferred, warning below (Bull's own invoices included).

« Pay with this wallet » pays only an invoice that passed every check above with no override (`walletPaymentFor(accepted:, status:, step:, now:)`: the invoice of the current status, never one a screen kept, with no warning on the status or the invoice): after « Show anyway », when the payee node is not the coordinator's known node, when the invoice expires within `TradeTolerances.walletPaymentMargin` (60 s, a guess until the first trials measure the swap), or on any trade whose coordinator once contradicted itself, only « Pay with another wallet » is offered. The port is given the exact BOLT11 string that was decoded and shown, never a string read again from the coordinator. The invoice and its step must not end within that margin, and its amount must be no more than what the user confirmed; any difference, or a new payment hash, cancels the payment and asks for a new confirmation.

### 4.5 App killed mid-trade, reopened

The trade is recoverable from the robot alone: the trade record gives (key, index, coordinator, order id); without it (file lost), the robot layer reads every bound robot back and finds `active_order_id` (§ 3.3). On reopen, the P2P tab lists « Active trades », and opening one resumes polling at once. A take whose answer was lost (the POST timed out): the trade record is written before the POST as « pending take » and the next GET of that order tells whether the robot is the pre-taker (`is_taker`) or not; a pending take older than the TAK timer with no pre-take is closed (the repository enforces the states, not the timer: #87 closes a pending take only after it). On reopen, the state machine resumes from the stored terms and trail (§ 3.5, #86): the payment hash of each step stays pinned and a breach stays remembered, whatever the coordinator answers after the restart. A trade for which the payments table (§ 3.5, added with the payment) shows a payment started is never paid again by the app: on reopen the screen shows that payment's state as the port reports it, and offers only « Pay with another wallet » for the same invoice until the port reports a failure. What is lost: the in-memory chat (read again from the coordinator) and the deadlines passed while closed (§ 3.8).

### 4.6 What the independent security reviewer checks in each pull request

- Package PRs (order, actions, chat, BOLT11, address): bounded parsing of every field; no coordinator text in failures; POST never retried; credential only in the robot's session; fixtures with no real token.
- Trade use cases: the money members called only there (architecture test); no agent or automated test calls them against a real coordinator; `cancel_status` always sent, as the stored status the answer implies (1 for a pre-take shown as 3); no automatic action on a surprising answer, the state machine's edges, amounts checks and their tolerances, the robot never reused, binding before first contact.
- Payout and chat: what is signed is exactly what was decoded and shown; the token refused in chat; the payout text refused in chat; the keyring's lifetime; the peer key pinned and named.
- UI: what the QR encodes, and what « Pay with this wallet » pays, is what was decoded and checked; nothing is paid without the confirmation tap in the trade screen, which states the amount and the quoted fees; the payment's progress is shown as the port reports it; the PIN before « Confirm received »; the chat panel protected from capture; no log.
- Schema 3: migration with rows, no update or delete of bindings, trade record written before the POST.

Each such PR is labelled « touches money » or « touches key material » and gets at least one independent review pass with no prior context, as for phase 1.

## 7. Open decisions

**For the coordinator (technical, recommendation first):**

1. Keep the unlocked robot key for the life of the trade screen (`TradeKeyring`), not one S2K per action (§ 3.4).
2. Write the trade record before the take's POST (« pending take ») so that a lost answer is recoverable (§ 4.5).
3. Send `cancel_status` with every cancel, taker included, set to the stored status that the answer shown implies: 1 for a pre-take shown as 3, the status shown otherwise (deviation from the reference, whose taker sends no guard, § 2.1: with the guard, a pre-taker's cancel that lands after the bond locked is refused with 1020 instead of losing the bond).
4. Tolerances of the amount checks: 2 % on price and bond, +1 sat on fees, to tune after the first trials (§ 3.2).
5. Polling: the reference's cadence, ×5 off-screen, nothing in the background (§ 3.8).
6. BOLT11: reuse the shell's pinned `bolt11_decoder` behind a port rather than a hand-written decoder; verify in PR 4 whether it recovers the payee key; if not, compare the payee only when the invoice states it.
7. Protect the chat panel from screen capture; leave invoice panels unprotected (§ 4.3).
8. The taker as seller refused until slice 5 (§ 2.1), so that no bond is locked for a step Troc cannot do.
9. Open the dispute from Troc in slice 4 (the reference's dialog and 6-hour wait), statements in phase 4 with the coordinator's contacts shown meanwhile (§ 2.7).

**For the owner (plain words):**

1. **The chat with users of the official app.** By default they will not see Troc's messages unless Troc also sends them through the coordinator's own Nostr relay (over Tor), as the official app does. Recommended: yes, do it in phase 2 (adds 1 to 2 days to the chat). Otherwise the chat works only with peers who changed a setting.
2. **How the buyer receives the sats**: paste an invoice or address from any wallet (as RoboSats) first, then « use this wallet ». Recommended order: Lightning invoice first, on-chain second, because on-chain is impossible below about 21,000 sats after fees and many coordinators disable it; the wallet's own Lightning invoices expire early (a new one may be asked at the end).
3. **Trading without a backup of the robots.** Recommended: refuse a trade on mainnet until the Garage Key has been shown and confirmed as saved (unless keys come from the wallet's 12 words, #81).
4. *(Dropped: there is no approval gate, by the owner's decision of 2026-11-13, § 3.7.)*
5. **What the app says about closing it during a trade**: a line on every waiting screen, « Troc does not notify you yet. Keep it open, or come back before {time}: otherwise you may lose your bond. », plus the next deadline in the P2P tab. Optionally, keep the screen awake while a trade screen is open.
6. **Amounts for the real trials**: the owner's choice at each trial, small ones. The cheapest known path for slice 1 is in § 2.0: a decoy sell order at the coordinator's minimum, bond 2 %, cancelled after the bond locks, which loses about half the bond (the other half comes back as rewards to the decoy's robot).
7. **« Confirm received » with the PIN** on mainnet (recommended), since it releases the escrow for good.
8. **Pasted keys' robots** may finish an order they already hold, never start a new one (recommended, in line with #79).
9. **The PIN before « Pay with this wallet »** (open). Someone holding the unlocked phone with the app open could take their own order, pay the bond from this wallet, cancel after the bond locks and recover half of it as rewards on their own maker robot, again and again (§ 4.1). Option A: the wallet's PIN before every « Pay with this wallet » on mainnet, as before « Confirm received » (the reviewer's recommendation). Option B: the confirmation tap alone, as the wallet does for its own sends if it asks no PIN. What will inform it: what the wallet itself asks before its own sends, found out by the #100 study; if the wallet sends without a PIN, the same person can already empty it from the wallet's own screens.
